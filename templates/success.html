🌟 Gaylord's journey

I began my educational journey at Daisy School in 2014, supported through HUG sponsorship via Daisy. It was here that I not only received quality education but also learned strong values and morals that continue to guide me today. In the same year, I lost my father, an event that shook our family deeply and left our academic journey uncertain. However, thanks to the sponsorship through Daisy and HUG, I was lifted during that difficult time. The sponsors did not just support me financially; they gave me hope, encouragement, and a firm foundation to continue pursuing my dreams. Through their support, I successfully completed my primary education and sat for my KCPE, where I scored above average marks that opened the door to one of the best schools in Kenya, Friends School Chavakali High School. From 2019 to 2022, my high school journey was smooth and fulfilling, made possible through the consistent support of the Daisy sponsors. I graduated with an above average grade, which qualified me to join one of the top universities in Kenya, Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT), where I am currently pursuing a degree in Analytical Chemistry. The sponsors also recognized and nurtured my passion for technology by funding me to take a Software Development course through BYU Pathway. This opportunity has broadened my horizons and allowed me to dream even bigger, balancing my love for both science and technology. Beyond the classroom, Team HUG walked with me and my family during the holidays, ensuring we had basic needs that sustained us when schools were closed. They even colored our festive seasons, especially Christmas, making our celebrations joyful and memorable. These moments of care and kindness gave me strength and reminded me that I was not alone in this journey. They became a source of motivation, fueling my determination to work harder and build a brighter future for myself and my family. Looking back, I am filled with deep gratitude. Daisy sponsorship through HUG has not only shaped my academic path but has also molded my character, instilling in me discipline, hard work, and resilience. To the rising generation under sponsorship, I encourage you to remain focused, appreciate the opportunity you’ve been given, and use it to transform your lives and communities. To the sponsors, I say thank you for walking this journey with me. Your support has changed my story, and I believe it will continue to change the lives of many others

Warm regards, Gaylord Ndenga
